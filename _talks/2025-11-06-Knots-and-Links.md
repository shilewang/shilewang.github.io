This is the "3+X" seminar on Knots and Links, organized by Prof. Yi Liu and Prof. Jiajun Wang in 2025 Fall at Peking University. The reference is Rolfsen's *Knots and Links*.

I presented part of Chapter 3 of *Knots and Links*, focusing on constructing knotted 2-spheres from knots and computing its fundamental groups.
