This is the "3+X" seminar on CAT(0) Cube Complexes, organized by Prof. Yi Liu, Prof. Jiajun Wang, and Prof. Wenyuan Yang in 2026 Spring at Peking University. The reference is mainly P. Schwer's *CAT(0) Cube Complexes: An Introduction*.

I presented part of Crisp and Wiest's result on how to embed right-angled Artin groups into mapping class groups. The reference is J. Crisp and B. Wiest's *Quasi-isometrically embedded subgroups of braid and diffeomorphism groups*.[PDF](/files/Quasi-isometrically-embedded-subgroups-of-braid-and-diffeomorphism-groups.pdf)
