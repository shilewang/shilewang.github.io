I introduced the group actions on trees, and discussed which groups must act with a fixed point on every tree. A central example is $$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$$, which can act on a tree without fixed points, while $$\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb{Z})$$ always fixes a point.
